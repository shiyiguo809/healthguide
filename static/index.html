Healthy Living Tips

Practical advice for staying healthy and active.

🚶

Stay Active Daily

Aim for at least 30 minutes of gentle movement each day. Even a slow walk helps heart health, balance, and mood.

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink 6–8 glasses of water daily. Older adults often feel less thirsty, but hydration is critical for all body functions.

😴

Prioritize Sleep

Aim for 7–9 hours per night. Good sleep reduces fall risk, supports memory, and keeps your immune system strong.

🥗

Balanced Nutrition

Include fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins. Limit salt and sugar to protect heart and kidney health.

🩺

Regular Check-ups

See your doctor at least once a year. Early detection of changes in blood pressure, cholesterol, and blood sugar saves lives.

🧠

Mental Wellness

Stay socially connected. Loneliness is as harmful as smoking. Call a friend, join a club, or talk to your AI assistant!

🫁

Breathing Exercises

Practice slow, deep breathing for 5 minutes each morning. It lowers blood pressure and reduces anxiety naturally.

⚖️

Healthy Weight

Maintaining a stable, healthy weight reduces joint pain and lowers risk of diabetes and heart disease.

🌞

Vitamin D

Get 15–20 minutes of sunlight or take a Vitamin D supplement. It supports bone strength and immune health.
